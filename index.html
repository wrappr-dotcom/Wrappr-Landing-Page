How it works

Three steps, no fixed hampers

Set once. Employees take it from there.

Step 1

Set a budget

Decide a per-employee amount for the occasion — Diwali, work anniversaries, or milestones.

Step 2

They choose

Employees pick from a curated catalog of brands within budget — no more guessing sizes or tastes.

Step 3

Upgrade, optionally

Want something above budget? Employees can top up the difference themselves and keep the gift.

The upgrade engine

A ₹1,000 company budget doesn't have to cap the gift. If an employee wants a ₹1,600 pair of earphones, they add ₹600 and it's theirs — you still only pay the budget you set.
